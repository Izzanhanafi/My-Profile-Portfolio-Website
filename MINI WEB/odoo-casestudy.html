Telecom Logistics ERP

Digital transformation for PT. Artha Telco Solusi (Simulated). Implementing Odoo ERP to streamline regional warehouse (STO) logistics, ensuring real-time asset accuracy and operational efficiency.

Role ERP Functional Consultant
Method SDLC, BPMN Mapping
Platform Odoo ERP Community

Operational Pain Points

Through an As-Is business process observation, several critical bottlenecks were identified in the existing logistics management:

  • Inaccurate Reporting: Lack of automated export features during Cycle Counting led to heavy reliance on error-prone manual data entry.
  • Traceability Gap: Weak tracking systems for reverse logistics (returned goods) caused discrepancies between physical stock and system records.
  • UI/UX Rigidity: The legacy system's interface slowed down administrators during high-speed scanning operations.

The SDLC Approach

I utilized the Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC) framework to ensure the engineered solution precisely answered the business needs:

  • System Design (To-Be Process): Mapped an Integrated Task Flow using BPMN to standardize receiving, distribution, and stock opname.
  • Configuration: Deployed Odoo ERP Community Edition, integrating Unique Serial Number tracking for hardware.
  • Validation: Conducted end-to-end logistics scenario testing to validate data integrity and reporting functionality.

Odoo System Configuration

Solving traceability and reporting issues through targeted module implementation.

End-to-End Asset Traceability

Activated the Unique Serial Number feature to guarantee 100% accuracy in tracking device history—from vendor inbound, distribution, to reverse logistics.

Automated Stock Opname

Configured the Inventory Adjustment module to eliminate manual recording, featuring instant dynamic exports to Excel formats for immediate audit readiness.
